Who We Are

A foundation built on love, driven by purpose, and committed to giving every child in Sierra Leone the chance to thrive.

Born From Love, Built For Change

The Destiny John Foundation was created to honor a beautiful soul and transform grief into lasting impact.

Destiny John was more than a name — they were a beacon of light, a reminder of the preciousness of every child's life. When we lost them, we made a promise: their memory would become a movement.

Today, we stand as a registered non-profit organization working hand-in-hand with communities across Sierra Leone to ensure no child is left behind.

"Every child we help is a piece of Destiny's legacy living on — a smile saved, a life protected, a future secured."

Our Journey So Far

The Foundation is Born

Destiny John Foundation is officially established with a clear, unwavering mission to reduce under-5 child mortality and bring hope to families across Sierra Leone.

2024

First Medical Outreach

Our team hit the ground running, conducting our first free pediatric health screening and reaching over 200 children in underserved communities.

2025

Healthcare Worker Training

We launched our frontline mentorship program, empowering and training 50+ local nurses and community health workers to provide sustainable care.

2025

Growing Our Impact

Expanded our reach to 25+ communities, launched comprehensive education support programs, and built vital partnerships with local hospitals.
