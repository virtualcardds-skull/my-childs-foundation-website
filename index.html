Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes! The Destiny John Foundation is a registered non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law, and you will receive an official tax receipt via email.

How is my donation used?

We are committed to transparency. 100% of your donation goes directly to our programs, including pediatric medical interventions, healthcare worker training, and educational support for children in Sierra Leone.

Can I volunteer with the foundation?

Absolutely! We welcome volunteers, especially those with medical, educational, or community development backgrounds. Please visit our Contact Us page to inquire about volunteer opportunities.

How can I partner with the Destiny John Foundation?

We love collaborating with like-minded organizations, businesses, and government bodies. Please reach out to us at info@destinyjohnfoundation.org to discuss partnership opportunities.
